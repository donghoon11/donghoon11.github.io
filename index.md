## About
I’m a graduate student at KAIST working across AI for design and robotics. 
Recent work includes simulation-to-real control for mobile robots, generative design of lightweight frames, and a LangGraph-based multi-agent pipeline (COALA) for imitation learning.  
I like building end-to-end systems—from CAD & simulation to data, learning, and deployment on Jetson.

## Research Interests
- AI for design & topology optimization (SIMP, generative structures)
- Robotics learning (VLA, imitation & diffusion policy)
- Sim-to-real pipelines, evaluation & datasets
- DfAM and lightweight structural design
